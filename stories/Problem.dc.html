The dominant model in music merchandise works against the people it claims to serve

The Advance Model Up Close

Advances are short-term cash for artists, but they give up rights to quality, pricing strategy, availability and value. The advance givers prioritise recouping their own costs — and keeping the artist in recoupment to them for longer.
